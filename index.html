I'm Aditya, a computer science student in New Delhi, graduating in 2026 — creative at heart who just happens to write code.

What gets me excited is the space where art, music, and technology collide. I think code and AI can be a creative medium just as much as a paintbrush or a piano, and I'm endlessly drawn to human-computer interaction — making machines feel a little more alive, a little more expressive.

Right now I build with AI, computer vision, and interactive systems. My favorite projects sit right at the edge of art and engineering, where the line between the two gets beautifully blurry — like teaching a webcam to read a hand and turn it into music.

I'm currently interning at Trendsoft Technologies, leading a UI/UX redesign from the ground up.
