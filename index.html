About Me

I'm Aditya, a CS student in New Delhi, graduating in 2026. Creative at heart who just happens to write code.

What gets me excited is the space where art, music, and technology collide. I believe code and AI can be a creative medium just as much as a paintbrush or a piano. I'm deeply drawn to human-computer interaction and how we can make machines feel more alive, more expressive, more human.
